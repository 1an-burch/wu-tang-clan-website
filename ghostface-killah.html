Ghostface Killah, or Dennis Coles, is one of the most prominent successes of Wu-Tang Clan. His fast-paced flow and intricate lyrical style on Wu-Tang’s debut album caught the attention of music critics, launching his solo career to spectacular heights. Ghostface found success with his debut album Ironman, and has continued to find success in rapping as well as music production in the hip hop scene.

Releasing 13 studio albums since Ironman, Ghostface has continued his commercial success all the way into the 2020s. About.com labeled him as one of the Top 50 MCs of Our Time, citing his skill in storytelling through his lyrics.

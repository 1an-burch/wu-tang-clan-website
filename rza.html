RZA, born Robert Fitzgerald Diggs, is a rapper and leader of Wu-Tang Clan. He is also a producer, producing most of the albums for the group, and being named by The Source as one of the 20 greatest producers of all time. RZA is the cousin of other members GZA and Ol’ Dirty Bastard.

RZA also is involved in filmmaking, scoring films such as Kill Bill: Volume 1 and Kill Bill: Volume 2. His directorial debut was The Man with the Iron Fists in 2012.

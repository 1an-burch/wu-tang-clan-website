Lamont Jody Hawkins, also known as U-God, or the Universal God, was another founding member of Wu-Tang Clan. Hawkins based his rap alias off his name in the black nationalist group Five-Percent Nation: “Universal God Allah.” Due to his conviction of firearm and drug possession in 1992, U-God was mostly unable to feature on Wu-Tang’s debut album, Enter the Wu-Tang. However, he was a main featuring member on the groups second album, Wu-Tang Forever.

U-God was able to have a semi-successful solo rap career, with his album Golden Arms Redemption going gold in 1999. Due to this success, Hawkins used his momentum to open his own recording label and release another album, Mr. Xcitement, shortly after.

Clifford Smith Jr., also known as Method Man, is a talented rapper, producer, and actor. The Wu-Tang rapper was one of the only members of the group with a solo track on their debut album: Enter the Wu-Tang (36 Chambers). As one of the more visible members of Wu-Tang, Method Man found extra success in the New York rap scene, featuring on The Notorious B.I.G.’s debut album, Ready to Die.

Method Man’s influence in the film industry has persisted since the 1990s as well, as he has appeared on the soundtracks of famous films such as Batman Forever. Smith also appeared as an actor in many films, including Keanu (2016). However, his best measure of success is the Grammy award he won in 1996 for Best Rap Performance by a Duo or Group with R&B singer Mary J. Blige.
